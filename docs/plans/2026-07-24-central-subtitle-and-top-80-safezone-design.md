# Design: Central Subtitle Layer & Top 80% UI Safe Zone Constraint

**Date:** 2026-07-24  
**Status:** Approved  
**Topic:** Transition subtitle rendering out of AI-generated TSX components into a central outer Remotion subtitle layer (`<DynamicSubtitle />`), while strictly constraining generated visual content to the top 80% height of the screen.

---

## 1. Problem Statement

1. **Inconsistent AI Subtitle Generation**: Forcing Gemini to generate 40-50 lines of subtitle timing and karaoke math inside each TSX file consumed ~40% of Gemini's token output budget, causing inconsistent subtitle rendering, missing subtitles, or syntax errors.
2. **Subtitle Collision Risks**: Without strict top-80% height constraints, visual elements generated by AI could overflow into the bottom 20% zone, colliding with subtitles.

---

## 2. Proposed Architecture & System Design

### Component A: Restrict AI Content to Top 80% Screen Height (`aiGen.js`)
- In `backend/services/aiGen.js`:
  - Update `systemInstruction` to instruct Gemini to wrap all visual content inside a container constrained to the top 80% screen height:
    ```tsx
    <div style={{
      position: "absolute",
      top: 0,
      left: 0,
      width: "100%",
      height: "80%",
      display: "flex",
      flexDirection: "column",
      justifyContent: "center",
      alignItems: "center",
      padding: "60px 60px 0 60px",
      boxSizing: "border-box",
      zIndex: 10
    }}>
    ```
  - Remove requirement for Gemini to generate subtitle code inside TSX files. Tell Gemini subtitles are handled by the outer video engine.

### Component B: Central Outer Subtitle Layer (`SceneWrapper` & `MainComposition.tsx`)
- In `StudioAIGen.jsx` (`SceneWrapper`) & `MainComposition.tsx`:
  - Render the central `<DynamicSubtitle />` layer in the reserved bottom 20% safe zone (`bottom: 12%` / `bottom: 160px`).
  - Read `subtitlesJson` / `voiceoverTtsJson` to display sentence-by-sentence karaoke highlights with zero black background box and strong text shadows.

---

## 3. Verification Plan
1. **Layout Safe Zone Test**:
   - Verify generated scene TSX wraps visual elements in top 80% height container.
2. **Outer Subtitle Test**:
   - Verify single-scene preview (`SceneWrapper`) and master preview (`MainComposition`) render crisp, synchronized subtitles in the bottom 20% zone across 100% of scenes without overlap.
